   
1. **Gradual Transition:**
    - **First Pass (English Subtitles):** Watch an episode with English subtitles. This helps you understand the dialogue while seeing how words are used in context.Start with English subtitles. This will help you associate spoken words with their written forms, improving your vocabulary and listening skills. As you become more comfortable, you'll start recognizing patterns and phrases.
    - **Second Pass (No Subtitles):** Rewatch the same episode without subtitles. This reinforces your listening skills without the crutch of text.
    - **Third Pass (Chinese Subtitles, if needed):** If there are still parts you don't understand, you can watch it with Chinese subtitles to ensure you fully grasp the content. However, use this sparingly to avoid becoming reliant on them.
2. **Active Listening:** Actively try to mimic the actors' speech patterns and intonations. Pause and repeat lines out loud to practice your speaking.
    
3. **Daily Vocabulary Practice:** Keep a notebook for new words and phrases. Write down unfamiliar terms, look up their meanings, and practice using them in sentences.
    
4. **Use Additional Resources:** Consider using language learning apps like Duolingo or Anki for daily vocabulary practice. Listening to English podcasts or audiobooks on topics of interest can also enhance your listening skills.
    
5. **Engage in Conversations:** If possible, find language exchange partners or join English-speaking groups online or in your community. Practice speaking as much as possible.
